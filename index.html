ABOUT

Hey, I'm Justice.

I'm a video editor who got into this because I kept noticing how often good content lost people in the first few seconds. That problem became the thing I obsess over. How a cut, a caption, or a single zoom can be the difference between someone staying or scrolling past.

Every edit I take on starts with one question. Will this hold someone past the first few seconds. That's the lens I bring to every talking-head video, every short-form cut, every caption placement.
